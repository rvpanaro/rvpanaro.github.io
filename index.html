Hi! I'm Renato Panaro, a PhD student in Biostatistics at the Department of Medical Statistics, University Medical Center Göttingen. I work on meta-analysis of clinical trials, survival analysis, and reproducible statistical methods. I'm based in Göttingen, Germany.

If you'd like to learn more about what I do, you might find these useful:

  • spsurv (R package, CRAN) — Semi-parametric survival analysis: proportional hazards, proportional odds, and accelerated failure time models for time-to-event data.
  • bsem (R package) — Bayesian structural equation modeling with Stan: confirmatory factor analysis and path models.
  • interactmeta (R package) — Contribution-adjusted meta-analysis (CAMS) for treatment effect modification from modifier-level aggregate data; Bayesian and REML inference.
  • Google Scholar — Publications and preprints on subgroup meta-analysis and related methods.
  • About — Full profile, expertise, teaching, and contact.
