Background

I am a PhD student in Biostatistics at the Department of Medical Statistics, University Medical Center Göttingen (UMG). From 2021 to 2025 I was a Research Associate in the same department. Most of my time goes to meta-analysis of clinical trials (subgroup comparisons and treatment-by-subgroup effects), time-to-event methods, and R code that others can rerun.

Topics I work on regularly: survival analysis, Bayesian inference, meta-analysis, clinical trial biometry, and statistical programming in R.

Software

  • spsurv — Semi-parametric survival analysis (PH, PO, AFT) with Bernstein-polynomial baselines; ML or Bayesian estimation via Stan. Package site. See Research (Mayrink & Demarqui).
  • bsem — Bayesian SEM with rstan (CFA, path models, Shiny app). CRAN (archived release); docs. See Research (Mayrink & Costa).
  • interactmeta — Contribution-adjusted meta-analysis (CAMS) for modifier-level aggregate data. Package site. See Research (Röver & Friede).
