Background

I am a PhD student in Biostatistics at the Department of Medical Statistics, University Medical Center Göttingen (UMG). From 2021 to 2025 I was a Research Associate in the same department. My dissertation is on contribution-adjusted meta-analysis of subgroup-specific trial data; the methods are in the interactmeta R package. I also work on time-to-event methods and other R software. I have a bachelor’s degree in Statistics from the Universidade Federal Fluminense (UFF) and a master’s in Statistics and Probability from the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG).

Topics I work on regularly: survival analysis, Bayesian inference, meta-analysis, clinical trial biometry, and statistical programming in R.

Software

  • spsurv — Semi-parametric survival analysis (PH, PO, AFT) with Bernstein-polynomial baselines; ML or Bayesian estimation via Stan. Package site. See Research (Mayrink & Demarqui).
  • bsem — Bayesian SEM with rstan (CFA, path models, Shiny app). CRAN (archived release); docs. See Research (Mayrink & Costa).
  • interactmeta (R package) — Contribution-adjusted meta-analysis (CAMS) for modifier-level aggregate data; main software from my PhD. Package site. See Research (Röver & Friede).

Research

With Röver and Friede: two papers accepted in Research Synthesis Methods; one manuscript (preprint) on interaction heterogeneity priors; and the package interactmeta (docs), main software from my PhD.

With Mayrink and Demarqui: one manuscript on semi-parametric time-to-event analysis; and the package spsurv on CRAN (docs).

With Mayrink and Costa: one paper in AStA Advances in Statistical Analysis (energy-sector SEM application); and the package bsem (CRAN, docs).

I received a young researchers’ presentation award at the symposium “Recent Advances in Meta-Analyses” (TU Dortmund) and have given conference talks at CEN2023 and ISPOR Europe 2026. Titles and links are on Google Scholar.

Tools
